❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💰 What is a Mutual Fund?+
A mutual fund pools money from many investors to invest in stocks, bonds, or other securities. It is managed by professional fund managers.
📊 What is SIP?+
SIP - Systematic Investment Plan allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. You can start with as low as ₹500 per month.
🛡️ Is my money safe in Mutual Funds?+
Mutual funds are regulated by SEBI and are subject to market risks. Diversification and professional management help reduce risk.
💵 What is ELSS and its tax benefit?+
ELSS is a tax-saving mutual fund with 3-year lock-in. Under Old Regime, you can claim deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80C.
🏧 How to withdraw money?+
You can redeem units anytime for open-ended funds. Money is credited within 1-3 working days. For ELSS, after 3 years lock-in.

📊 INVESTMENT PORTFOLIO

Whenever you think of making investment, three things should come on top of your mind – Risk Profiling, Products and Asset Allocation. Along with these – cash-flow situations, investments already made, net-worth are equally important.

📈
RISK PROFILING –
Knowing ourselves well is more important than choosing products first. Risk Profiling helps in doing that.
💼
PRODUCTS –
There are many investment products available. First check should be that product must be regulated by govt. appointed regulatory body.
⚖️
ASSET ALLOCATION –
We should not put all eggs in one basket. Distributing investments into poorly correlated asset classes saves us from big losses.

🎯 TARGETED INVESTMENT

Making holistic investment choice is always preferred than making random investments. Every investment should be linked to a certain purpose – retirement, child’s higher education, marriage etc.

The key to accumulate retirement fund in time is to start at earliest. You can start with small amounts and increase it as your salary increases.

💰 TAX OPTIMIZATION

Tax planning involves optimizing your wealth creation while aligning with tax laws. Tax planning should never be done in isolation. We prioritize paying minimum possible tax.

•
Creation of long-term wealth
•
Taking life insurance cover
•
Health insurance cover and health check-up
•
Availing home loan for buying property
•
Donating for a charitable cause
